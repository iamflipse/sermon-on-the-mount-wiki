Verses the lecture does not read aloud

Peterson reads the sermon almost verse by verse. Checking each KJV verse against the transcript, these five are never read out or discussed on their own:

  • Matthew 5:9, "Blessed are the peacemakers." The reading jumps from 5:8 to 5:10. Peacemaking comes up later, under Love Your Enemies.
  • Matthew 5:12, "Rejoice, and be exceeding glad... for so persecuted they the prophets." The idea is covered in the discussion of 5:10–11.
  • Matthew 5:45, "He maketh his sun to rise on the evil and on the good." Peterson reads 5:44 and 5:46–48 around it.
  • Matthew 7:8, "For every one that asketh receiveth." Covered by the discussion of 7:7.
  • Matthew 7:18, "A good tree cannot bring forth evil fruit." Covered by the discussion of 7:17 and 7:19–20.

Smaller skips: 5:1–2 is treated as the setting, and the first half of 6:32 ("for after all these things do the Gentiles seek") is passed over. Every other verse is read aloud and commented on.
