## Peterson's reading in plain words

The people were astonished at both what was said and how it was said. Peterson describes astonishment as being overwhelmed by the intensity of the light. Christ taught "as one having authority, and not as the scribes": the authority of someone who lives what he says, set against mere rule-keeping. With this the series ends.

## Key ideas

- 'This is an amazing line, too.'
- They could not believe what they heard or the manner in which it was delivered.
- To be astonished is to be overwhelmed by the intensity of the light.
- The phrase tells you about the crowd's reaction.
- He taught as one having authority, not as the scribes: authority versus mere rule-keeping (see the scribes and Pharisees section).
