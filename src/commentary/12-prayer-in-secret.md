## Peterson's reading in plain words

Church, Peterson says, is for penitents, not for status. The private prayer he recommends is a question: what am I doing that is stupid and wrong, that I could change, and that I would change? Ask it honestly and the answer comes. Start small: a client who could not face cleaning his room began with a quarter of one sock drawer, and progress then grew quickly.

On vain repetitions, he says God is not impressed by volume. Real prayer is close to real thinking: you ask a genuine question and an answer appears that you did not have before. The more genuine the question, the better the answer. But answers must be tested, so bring in people who disagree and let them hammer on your idea.

## Key ideas

**From lecture section 26: Enter Into Thy Closet: Private Prayer and Small Steps** (Matthew 6:5-6)

- The Jesus prayer reminds you that you are a sinner, not a moral paragon; church is a penitentiary.
- Going to church despite doctrinal doubts signals willingness to subordinate yourself to a shared enterprise.
- The simple prayer: what am I doing that is stupid and wrong, that I could change, that I would change.
- In an argument, ask what you did to make it likely, even if the other person is 95 percent wrong.
- The client left the vacuum diagonally across the threshold for a week; the hero first meeting the giant often runs.
- Start with something humble enough: one quarter of one sock drawer; progress then becomes exponential.

**From lecture section 27: Use Not Vain Repetitions: Thinking as Prayer** (Matthew 6:7-8)

- God is not impressed by sheer volume of communication.
- Your Father knows what you need; the task is to open yourself to revelation.
- If you think, you pray: you ask a question and get an answer you did not have.
- Jung: thoughts appear in the theater of imagination like a table when you walk into a room.
- Do not write an essay about something you do not care about; it is a lie from the beginning.
- Paranoid people are deluded by the force of their own thoughts; revelation must be tested.
