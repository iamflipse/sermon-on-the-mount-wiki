## Peterson's reading in plain words

Strait means narrow and confined, not straight. Peterson takes it to mean that in the end there is one right way, pointing toward a kind of unity that is very hard to hit exactly. His image is a band improvising with its audience, everyone in the same place at once, or dancing properly to the music. That shared moment is the narrow gate. It is rare because it is the highest thing there is.

## Key ideas

- Strait means confined, narrow; there is one right way in the final analysis.
- It points to something like a divine unity, very hard to attain exactly.
- Like dancing to the music properly, or a band improvising and cooking with the audience.
- That shared moment is the line between yin and yang, a line of meaning.
- It is something to see, not to conceptualize precisely.
- It is rare because it is the highest possible thing.
