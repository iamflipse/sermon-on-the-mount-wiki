## Peterson's reading in plain words

Salt preserves food, but above all it adds flavour: it turns something good into something delightful. Peterson says the same savour shows in Christ's quick, truthful answers to trick questions, in comedians who have to tell the truth to be funny, and in the court fool, the one person free to say what is so. Anyone who is an enemy of the fool is a tyrant.

The city on a hill joins the mountain image to community: you climb so you can join others who have climbed. Treating your future self well and treating other people well turn out to be the same skill. A candle on a candlestick means being good in a way that makes others want to copy you. Light draws attention and attention leads to imitation, so do good in the open when people need the example, not to show off. He closes with a wedding custom in which a candle held between the couple shows that the light above both of them, the truth that comes from honest talk, is what is in charge of a marriage.

## Key ideas

**From lecture section 9: You Are the Salt of the Earth** (Matthew 5:13)

- Roman soldiers were paid in salt; salt is vital and preserves.
- Salt also gives savor, turning what is good into what is delightful.
- Asked which commandment is greatest, Christ abstracts the core of the law, and no one dares ask more.
- Comedy must be truthful; you laugh before you think, so the truth hits you in the body.

**From lecture section 10: You Are the Light of the World: The City on the Hill** (Matthew 5:14)

- The city on the hill is Jerusalem; you climb to join a community that has climbed.
- There is no difference between being good to yourself and being a shining light for the community.
- You are a community across time; treating your future selves properly equals treating others properly.
- Psychopaths treat people as fruit trees and betray their own future selves the same way.

**From lecture section 11: A Candle on a Candlestick: Good Works and the Father** (Matthew 5:15-16)

- 'Let your light so shine' means be so good that people want to be like you.
- Light attracts attention like a moth to a flame, and attention compels imitation; the halo and the gold coin carry the same idea.
- The proper jihad is a competition between invitations, not conversion by the sword.
- If what you are doing is good, do it in public because people need the example, not to brag.

**From lecture section 12: The Candle Between Two People, Imitation and Awe** (Matthew 5:14-16 (continued))

- The light between the spouses, the truth from honest dialogue, is what is superordinate in a marriage.
- Eve means 'beneficial adversary'; opponent processing gives precision, like two muscles moving a hand smoothly.
- Treat your partner as you; you are stuck with each other.
- Christianity is the imitation of Christ: a pattern to act out, not a set of propositions.

## Questions to explore

- Where could you add savor rather than just be harmless?
- Which future self are you betraying today?
- Whose light do you imitate without noticing?
- What does your partner see that you do not, and do you let it correct you?
