## Peterson's reading in plain words

Peterson calls this "a rough one for modern people" and says plainly that he is not the authority to rule on whether divorce is allowed. What he offers is Jung's view that marriage only works as a real vow: if there is an escape route, you will take it at exactly the moment you should be changing instead.

He balances this with a case from his practice, a client married to someone willing to burn everything down, whom he spent years helping. His conclusion is not a rule about divorce but a description of commitment: if you are not all in, you are not married.
