## Resilience in particular

The word "resilience" never appears in the lecture, but it is one of its main subjects. The clearest places:

- [The Wise and Foolish Builders](commentary/26-wise-and-foolish-builders.md): "It's exposure. You can walk through all of that before it happens. And then when it happens, you're ready."
- [The Golden Rule](commentary/22-golden-rule.md): "celebratory acceptance. Bring it on."
- [Ask, Seek, Knock](commentary/21-ask-seek-knock.md): "You can obviously learn. And so there is more to you than there is now."
- [The Beatitudes](commentary/02-beatitudes.md): "Your stability starts to become dependent on your competence, and that's a way better deal."
- [The Lord's Prayer](commentary/13-lords-prayer.md): "The people you admire are definitely the people who take their burdens on voluntarily."
