# Cheat Sheet

The whole lecture series on one page. Quotes are Peterson's; everything else is paraphrase.

## The core idea

Peterson reads the Sermon on the Mount as practical guidance for living. Aim at the highest good you can conceive, admit what you do not know, tell the truth, and take on your burdens voluntarily. Fix your own faults before judging others, treat people the way you want to be treated, and prepare for catastrophe before it arrives, so that your life is built on rock.

## Seven main points

**1. Aim up.** Setting your sights on the highest good orients your whole life. ([The Setting](commentary/01-setting.md), 5:1)

> "And so, aim up. That's the first rule."

**2. Admit what you do not know.** Stability comes from competence, and competence starts with facing your ignorance. ([The Beatitudes](commentary/02-beatitudes.md), 5:3)

> "In order to move out into the world and to become competent, you have to make friends with what you don't know."

**3. Tell the truth.** Truthful speech is how you get the adventure that makes life worth it. ([The Beatitudes](commentary/02-beatitudes.md), 5:6)

> "What redeems your life is the adventure of your life. It's not happiness, it's not ease or security"

**4. Take on burdens voluntarily.** The same challenge works differently when you choose it. ([The Lord's Prayer](commentary/13-lords-prayer.md), 6:10)

> "The people you admire are definitely the people who take their burdens on voluntarily."

**5. Clear your own eye first.** Deal with your own blindness before correcting others. ([Judging](commentary/19-judging.md), 7:3–5)

> "you actually can't act as a moral social agent until you've cleaned the debris out of your own vision."

**6. Treat others as you want to be treated, and go first.** ([The Golden Rule](commentary/22-golden-rule.md), 7:12)

> "The price you pay is that you have to do that to other people, first."

**7. Build on the rock.** Facing the worst ahead of time builds a character that can withstand it. ([The Wise and Foolish Builders](commentary/26-wise-and-foolish-builders.md), 7:24–27)

> "It's exposure. You can walk through all of that before it happens. And then when it happens, you're ready."

## Seven practices

| Do this | Passage |
|---|---|
| Aim at the highest good you can see in each area of life: "aim at the highest possible good in every possible way." | [Do Not Worry](commentary/18-do-not-worry.md) (6:33) |
| Watch your words: notice whether each one makes you weaker or stronger, and stop saying the ones that weaken you. | [The Beatitudes](commentary/02-beatitudes.md) (5:6) |
| Choose your challenges instead of waiting for them to find you. | [The Lord's Prayer](commentary/13-lords-prayer.md) (6:10) |
| Reconcile before you worship: fix things with the people close to you first. | [Anger and Reconciliation](commentary/05-anger.md) (5:23–24) |
| Ask for what you want. Not asking makes the odds close to zero. | [Ask, Seek, Knock](commentary/21-ask-seek-knock.md) (7:7) |
| In an argument, work out what would actually satisfy you, and ask the other person. | [Oaths](commentary/08-oaths.md) (5:37) |
| Ask the closet prayer: what am I doing that is stupid and wrong, that I could change, that I would change? Then start with a quarter of one sock drawer. | [Prayer in Secret](commentary/12-prayer-in-secret.md) (6:6) |

## One-minute version

The sermon starts on a mountain because you should aim up. Aim at the highest good and that aim fills you with something like enthusiasm, which in Greek means being filled with God. Admit what you do not know, speak the truth, and pick up your burdens on purpose. Judge your own intent, not just your outcomes, and make peace with your brother before anything else. Do good in secret, put one aim at the top, and stop worrying. Look at your own faults before other people's, ask for what you want, and treat others how you want to be treated, starting first. Act out what you believe rather than just saying it. Face catastrophe before it comes and you are the house on the rock: the storm still comes, but you do not fall.

## Five quick questions

1. **If not happiness, what redeems a life?** "What redeems your life is the adventure of your life." ([The Beatitudes](commentary/02-beatitudes.md))
2. **Is forgiveness blanket absolution?** "It isn't blanket absolution; it's reciprocity." ([The Lord's Prayer](commentary/13-lords-prayer.md))
3. **Does "judge not" mean never judge?** "So it doesn't exactly say don't judge." ([Judging](commentary/19-judging.md))
4. **What is the best disguise of a predator?** "the most effective guise of a predator is compassion." ([False Prophets](commentary/24-false-prophets-and-fruits.md))
5. **When disaster hits, what is the real question?** "Is it because of the tragedy or is it because you weren't ready to withstand it?" ([The Wise and Foolish Builders](commentary/26-wise-and-foolish-builders.md))
