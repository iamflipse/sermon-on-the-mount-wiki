Resilience in particular

The word "resilience" never appears in the lecture, but it is one of its main subjects. The clearest places:

  • The Wise and Foolish Builders: "It's exposure. You can walk through all of that before it happens. And then when it happens, you're ready."
  • The Golden Rule: "celebratory acceptance. Bring it on."
  • Ask, Seek, Knock: "You can obviously learn. And so there is more to you than there is now."
  • The Beatitudes: "Your stability starts to become dependent on your competence, and that's a way better deal."
  • The Lord's Prayer: "The people you admire are definitely the people who take their burdens on voluntarily."
